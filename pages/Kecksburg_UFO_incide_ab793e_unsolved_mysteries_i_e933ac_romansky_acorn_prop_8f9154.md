## Introduction

The familiar “space acorn” associated with [the Kecksburg UFO incident]({{ 'the-kecksburg-ufo-incident/' | relative_url }}) owes much of its appearance to a single witness description. When *Unsolved Mysteries* dramatised the case in 1990, producers needed a physical object that viewers could see and remember. The most detailed visual account available came from volunteer fireman James Romansky, who claimed he had seen a large metallic object in the woods after the 1965 incident. His description of an acorn-shaped craft with unusual [markings]({{ 'markings/' | relative_url }}) became the foundation for the television prop that later evolved into Kecksburg’s best-known symbol.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. ## What Romansky Said He Saw

Among the various accounts connected to Kecksburg, Romansky’s stands out because of its specificity. In interviews later featured by *Unsolved Mysteries*, he described a large metallic object partly embedded in the ground. He estimated it was roughly eight to twelve feet long and several feet across. Most significantly, he said it looked “exactly like a fresh acorn” picked from a tree.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. On that bumper there was what I call, it...</span></span></span>

Romansky also emphasised what the object lacked. According to his recollection, there were no wings, engines, propellers or recognisable aircraft features. He therefore concluded that it did not resemble any conventional aircraft he knew.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. On that bumper there was what I call, it...</span></span></span>

Another detail that became central to later retellings involved markings around a lower band or rim. Romansky compared these [symbols]({{ 'symbols/' | relative_url }}) to ancient Egyptian [hieroglyphics]({{ 'hieroglyphics/' | relative_url }}), describing shapes, circles, lines and star-like forms unlike anything he had previously encountered. Whether those markings existed exactly as remembered is impossible to verify today, but the description became one of the most repeated elements in the Kecksburg narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. ## Which Details Became Visual Design Choices

When *Unsolved Mysteries* recreated the Kecksburg incident, the production team faced a practical challenge. Unlike modern documentaries, the programme relied heavily on physical reconstructions rather than computer-generated imagery. A prop had to communicate the witness story instantly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.roadsideamerica.com/story/16860" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: roadsideamerica.com">[RoadsideAmerica.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">roadsideamerica.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roadside America.com Space Acorn</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Space Acorn - 1965 UFO, Kecksburg, PennsylvaniaA UFO allegedly crashed in Kecksburg in 1965, and the town proudly displays a replica that...</span></span></span>

Several design choices appear to have come directly from Romansky’s description:

**The overall [shape]({{ 'shape/' | relative_url }}).** The prop was built with a tapered, acorn-like body rather than the classic flying-saucer form associated with UFO culture. This immediately distinguished Kecksburg from countless other UFO stories.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. On that bumper there was what I call, it...</span></span></span>

**The scale.** Romansky described something roughly the size of a small vehicle. The television reconstruction was therefore built as a large, life-sized object rather than a miniature model. Visitors and viewers could imagine standing beside it, which increased its impact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. On that bumper there was what I call, it...</span></span></span>

**The symbol band.** The prop included a ring of strange markings near its lower section, reflecting Romansky’s “hieroglyphic” description. This feature became one of the most recognisable visual elements of the Kecksburg image.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. On that bumper there was what I call, it...</span></span></span>

**The absence of conventional aircraft features.** Designers did not add wings, visible propulsion systems or obvious cockpit details. In this respect, the prop followed Romansky’s account as closely as practical for television.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://unsolved.com/gallery/kecksburg-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unsolved.com">[Unsolved Mysteries]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unsolved.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Mysteries Kecksburg UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Unsolved MysteriesKecksburg UFO - Unsolved MysteriesThere was a bumper on the bottom part of it. On that bumper there was what I call, it...</span></span></span>

## Where Witness Memory Ends and Television Interpretation Begins

The famous space acorn should not be mistaken for a documented photograph of an object recovered in 1965. It is a reconstruction derived from witness recollections, especially Romansky’s account. This distinction is crucial when evaluating the evidence.
