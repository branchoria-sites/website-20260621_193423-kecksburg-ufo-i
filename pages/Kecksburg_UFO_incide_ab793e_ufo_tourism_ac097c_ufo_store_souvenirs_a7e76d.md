## Introduction

The UFO Store is one of the most practical ways the Kecksburg UFO story becomes a local economic asset. While the annual [festival]({{ 'festival/' | relative_url }}) attracts large crowds, the store gives travellers a reason to stop throughout the year, buy a souvenir, and contribute to a community institution. Operated in connection with the Kecksburg Volunteer Fire Department, the shop turns an unresolved mystery into a steady stream of small purchases, repeat visits and fundraising support. ## Souvenirs as Proof of a Visit

For many visitors, the UFO Store functions much like the gift shop at a historic site or roadside attraction. A photograph beside the famous “Space Acorn” [replica]({{ 'replica/' | relative_url }}) is one form of evidence that someone visited Kecksburg; a T-shirt, sign, mug or other themed item becomes a physical reminder that can be taken home and shown to others. Atlas Obscura notes that UFO-themed merchandise became part of the attraction surrounding the replica object displayed near the fire department social hall.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/space-acorn" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: atlasobscura.com">[Atlas Obscura]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">atlasobscura.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">space acorn</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Atlas ObscuraSpace Acorn in Kecksburg3 Jul 2013 — Space Acorn replica on display in Kecksburg, PA Ryright. The importance of these [souvenirs]({{ 'souvenirs/' | relative_url }}) goes beyond their retail value. Merchandise extends the reach of the story after visitors leave town. A UFO shirt worn elsewhere or a novelty item displayed at home can prompt conversations about Kecksburg and encourage future tourism through word-of-mouth. In tourism economics, souvenirs often function as miniature advertisements, and the UFO Store gives Kecksburg a dedicated place to produce and distribute those reminders.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/space-acorn" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: atlasobscura.com">[Atlas Obscura]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">atlasobscura.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">space acorn</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Atlas ObscuraSpace Acorn in Kecksburg3 Jul 2013 — Space Acorn replica on display in Kecksburg, PA Ryright. ## Daily Retail Beyond the Festival Weekend

A challenge for many small-town attractions is maintaining visitor activity between major events. The UFO Store addresses that problem by operating independently of the annual festival. The Kecksburg Volunteer Fire Department advertises the store as open daily and also offers mail-order sales, allowing people who discover the story later to purchase merchandise even when they are not physically in Kecksburg.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://kecksburgvfd.com/ufo-store/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kecksburgvfd.com">[Kecksburg Volunteer Fire Department]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kecksburgvfd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Kecksburg Volunteer Fire Department UFO Store</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open daily 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM. ## Skeptics, Believers and Playful Merchandise

One reason the UFO Store works as a tourism attraction is that it does not require visitors to take sides in the debate over what happened in 1965.
